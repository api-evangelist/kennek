Unpacking the 30% Non-Accrual Crisis in UK Private Credit The UK private credit market is facing an alarming crisis as non-accrual rates surge to unprecedented levels. Recent data from market data platform Solve reveals that US business development companies (BDCs) reported nearly a third of their loan book failed to make payments in Q1 2025, amounting to a staggering £1.4 billion in troubled loans. More concerning still, 27% of these non-accruals represent new instances, with 54 BDCs reporting at least one new non-accruing loan in the first quarter [1].
