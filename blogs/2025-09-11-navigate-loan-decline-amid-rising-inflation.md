The UK bridging finance sector finds itself at a critical juncture as rising inflation and increased borrowing costs create significant headwinds for lenders. Recent data shows UK inflation has jumped to 3.5% from 2.6% in March, while government borrowing rose by £1 billion in April 2025 marking the fourth highest April borrowing since records began in 1993 [1]. This economic environment presents substantial challenges for bridging lenders who must now navigate declining loan volumes while maintaining profitability and market position.
