The Evolving Regulations for Non-Bank Lenders in 2025 As we move through 2025, non-bank lenders face an increasingly complex regulatory environment characterised by fragmentation and shifting enforcement priorities. While federal oversight through the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) has diminished, state regulators have stepped forward to fill the void, creating a patchwork of compliance requirements that vary significantly across jurisdictions.
