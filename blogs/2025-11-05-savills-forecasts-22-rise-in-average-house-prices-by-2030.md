📈 UK housing market momentum = opportunity for bridging lenders According to Savills , average UK house prices are forecast to rise by 22% by 2030, adding around £80,000 to the current average home value. While growth will be gradual—about 1% in 2025, 2% in 2026 and peaking at 5.5% in 2029—there’s a very clear upward trajectory. For UK bridging lenders especially, this paints an interesting landscape: As property values rise, loan-to-value ratios improve, reducing risk on bridging transactions.
